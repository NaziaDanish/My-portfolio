My Columns

Writing has always been my way of expressing the unspoken realities of our society. I write columns that shed light on the bitter truths we often ignore — issues like injustice, inequality, and the silent struggles of everyday people. Through my words, I aim to spark awareness and inspire positive change. These columns are not just pieces of writing; they are reflections of truth, emotion, and a voice for the unheard.
